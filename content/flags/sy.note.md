svg-country-flags still ships the pre-2024 Ba'athist flag (black, white and red bands with
two green stars). Syria adopted the three-star flag after the fall of the Assad government:
three equal bands, green over white over black, with three red five-pointed stars along the
centre of the white band, 2:3 (Article 6 of the Constitutional Declaration, 13 March 2025).
`content/` already described the current flag, so only the image was stale.

Provenance of the drawing: the geometry (bands of 200 in a 900x600 field; stars centred on
x = 225, 450, 675 with a 150-unit bounding height, centred on the white band) follows
Wikimedia's `Flag of Syria (2025-).svg`. The Declaration fixes only the band order, the
ratio and "three red stars"; it gives no colour values or star dimensions, so the shades
(#007a3d, #ce1126) are the conventional pan-Arab values that Wikimedia and flagcolorcodes.com
use, not an official specification. Delete this override once svg-country-flags ships the
current flag.
