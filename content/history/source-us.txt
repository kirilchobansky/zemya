# NOTES TO CHECK
# 1. Dates before 14.09.1752 are as commonly cited. Columbus (12.10.1492) is the Julian date; Jamestown (14.05.1607) is old style. Period boundaries from 1763 on are Gregorian.
# 2. Period boundaries are editorial choices, not official dates. The Reconstruction end (04.03.1877) is the day before Hayes's public inauguration; the Cold War end (26.12.1991) is the dissolution of the USSR.
# 3. Presidents with two lines (Cleveland, Trump) are the 22nd/24th and 45th/47th presidencies, so there are 47 lines but 45 different people.
# 4. Presidents who were never elected to the office: Tyler, Fillmore, Andrew Johnson, Arthur, Ford. Theodore Roosevelt, Coolidge, Truman and Lyndon Johnson took over after a death but later won their own election, so they are marked yes.
# 5. John Quincy Adams (House vote) and Hayes (disputed result) are marked yes because both were formally elected; the notes explain how.
# 6. Trump and Vance as the current president and vice president were confirmed by search; check again before you publish, since this is the only line that can change.
